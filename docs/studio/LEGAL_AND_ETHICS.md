# Protocol research legal and ethics boundary

This document is engineering policy, not legal advice.

- Capture only equipment and networks you own or are explicitly authorized to
  test. Authorization must cover the interface, time window and intended use.
- Follow local law, contracts, warranties, safety rules, export controls and
  applicable anti-circumvention restrictions.
- Do not use Studio to bypass access controls, licensing, metering, safety
  interlocks or manufacturer protections.
- Treat captures as potentially sensitive. Device identifiers, site topology,
  operational schedules and proprietary protocol content may be confidential.
- Mark NDA material at ingestion. It may be retained locally under the user's
  controls but cannot enter `.sdcap` exports, research bundles or contributions.
- Use clean-room procedures when required: separate observation from
  implementation, record sources and permissions, and retain evidence trails.
- Do not claim manufacturer endorsement, compatibility or hardware validation
  from synthetic data, document research or an inferred mapping.
- Report safety or security vulnerabilities responsibly to the affected vendor
  before public disclosure where appropriate.

Replay Lock is permanent for captured and derived frames in this repository.
Protocol findings begin as observations and require independent evidence and
review before promotion; hardware verification requires an approval engineer.
